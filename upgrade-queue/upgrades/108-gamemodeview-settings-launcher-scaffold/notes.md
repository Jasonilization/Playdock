# GameModeView: floating Settings launcher + overlay (scaffold)

Adds the SettingsLauncher storage-key namespace, the showSettingsLauncher / showingSettings state, and the not-yet-wired pieces: noDashboardOverlayActive, cornerFloaters, settingsOverlay (which instantiates SettingsPanelView), openSettings(), and the minimizable settingsFloatingLauncher pill (mirrors the Steam floating icon).

Applies on top of: 058-localization-engine, 107-settings-panel-scaffold
